## One unresolved measurement

The Indonesian source says “Kandungan air max 20%,” while its English equivalent says “Contains fresh coconut water.” The owner's general “yes” confirms publication approval but does not define what the 20% figure measures or its test method. The public website therefore uses the unambiguous fresh-coconut-water statement in both languages. The numeric 20% water limit remains off the public page until its measurement basis is supplied. Do not silently relabel it as moisture content, water weight, or coconut-water volume.
